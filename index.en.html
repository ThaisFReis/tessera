Preserve the choice.

What stays forever does not need to carry what must stay secret.

Tessera06 / Threat model

Trust begins with
explicit limits.

The protocol guarantees

  • Hiding the choice inside the published commitment — perfectly, not computationally.
  • Checking that your own vote was included.
  • Publicly verifying the final tally, at a cost constant in turnout.
  • Mixing open and secret questions in one ballot, in one transaction.
  • Blocking double voting and settling quorum in one phase.
  • Refusing to tally when the secret group holds fewer than five ballots — demonstrated on testnet, with the accumulators closing.

The protocol depends on

  • A board that destroys the copies of the ballots.
  • A private environment for the act of voting.
  • A result that does not expose everyone through unanimity.
  • A large enough secret group: if subtraction would expose it, the contract would rather not publish.
  • Unlinking issuer and commitment in a future stage.
Guarantees implemented and measured · limits declared, not discovered later07 / 14
